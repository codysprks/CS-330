# CS-330 Computational Graphics and Visualization

## How do I approach designing software?

Throughout this course, I learned that designing software requires planning, patience, and understanding how different components work together. For my final project, I created a 3D workspace using C++ and OpenGL that included a desk, cabinet, drawers, and flooring. My design process involved breaking down complex objects into smaller shapes and positioning them using transformations. One of my biggest challenges was getting objects to align correctly within the scene. Through trial and error, I learned how important it is to plan object placement and understand the relationship between the X, Y, and Z axes. Moving forward, I can apply this same approach to future projects by breaking larger problems into smaller, manageable tasks.

## How do I approach developing programs?

My approach to developing programs has evolved throughout this course. Initially, I focused on getting the basic shapes to render correctly before adding additional features. As the milestones progressed, I incorporated camera movement, textures, lighting, and more complex objects. One strategy that helped me was making small changes, running the program, and observing the results before continuing. There were several occasions when adjusting one object's position affected the overall appearance of my scene, which required additional troubleshooting. This iterative process helped me become more comfortable debugging code and understanding how individual changes affect the final product. I also gained more experience working with C++ and organizing different sections of my code.

## How can computer science help me in reaching my goals?

Computer science continues to provide me with valuable skills that I can apply throughout my educational and professional career. Although my primary interest is cybersecurity, this course introduced me to another area of software development that I had limited experience with. Working with computational graphics helped strengthen my problem-solving skills, programming knowledge, and understanding of how software interacts with hardware to produce visual results. These skills will be useful as I continue pursuing my Computer Science degree with a concentration in Information Security. Professionally, I believe the troubleshooting, testing, and analytical skills I developed throughout this course will transfer into cybersecurity and other technical roles. This project also reinforced the importance of continuous learning and adapting to new technologies.
